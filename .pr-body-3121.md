## Audit panel unchoke: senior role replaces the dead straitly seat; owner-authored issues bypass the panel; tally rejects keyword-only reasons

**fleet-ops#3121.** The senior-auditor admission panel was starved because the
`straitly` auditor role resolved only within a provider that is a 402 money
wall (`pi --print --provider straitly --model gpt-5.6-sol` -> `credit balance
exhausted`, Nish-reserved), so every `pi-audit@...--straitly` unit failed
instead of rotating to a live seat. This PR replaces the dead `straitly`
role everywhere with a `senior` role that reads a single ordered ladder
(`senior_seats_in_order` in `config/seat-caps.json`) and resolves to the
first usable seat — a walled seat is skipped, never a unit failure
(fleet-ops#3121, Nish 2026-09-04 correction: devin / free-glm / senior
three-role panel stays).

### What changed

- **config/seat-caps.json** — add `senior_seats_in_order`
  (`cursor/cursor-grok-4.6-high`, `xai-oauth/grok-4.6`,
  `openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`) and **delete**
  `keystone_only_providers` — one mechanism, not two (cursor stays
  keystone/senior-review via the hardcoded gate in seat-lib).
- **lib/seat-lib.sh** — parse `senior_seats_in_order` into `SEAT_SENIOR_ORDER`
  and add `find_senior_seat()`: first usable seat in the ladder wins; if the
  whole ladder is walled it falls through to any usable capable seat
  (a walled role resolves to its fallback instead of failing the unit). EOF:
  returns 1 only when nothing anywhere is usable right now (lane fault).
- **bin/pi-audit-run** — rename the `straitly` role to `senior` (keep
  `straitly` as a back-compat alias) and resolve it via the ladder; add the
  `free-glm` role (zenmux/z-ai/glm-5.3-free, cline/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, then
  any free-class seat, then any capable seat). No money-wall fail-loop: when
  every senior seat is walled, the role rotates rather than exits 1.
- **bin/fleet-heartbeat-auditor** — panel `ROLES=(devin free-glm senior)`;
  add the **owner-authored admission bypass**: a `scout-candidate` authored
  by `nish3451` whose body passes `lib/agent-ready-spec-gate.py` goes
  straight to `agent-ready` (no three-seat panel), one `OWNER-BYPASS` log
  line each.
- **bin/pi-escalation-audit** — panel `ROLES=(devin free-glm senior)`.
- **bin/fleet-gap-closure-conference** — resolve the third auditor
  (`DEFAULT_AUDITORS`) to the first-usable senior seat at start instead of
  the hardcoded dead `straitly`/ds4-pro.
- **bin/fleet-blind-audit** — the judge/reviewer seat prefers
  `find_senior_seat()` (senior ladder) and falls back to `pick_seat`.
- **bin/pi-audit-tally** — reject a `2-of-3 PASS` admission when any PASS
  reason is keyword-only with no repo-specific evidence (an issue number
  `#N`, a path, or a URL); `EVIDENCE-REFUSED`, no `agent-ready`. Keyword-only
  compliance is not a judgement.

`{"auditor":"senior","provider":"cursor","model":"cursor-grok-4.6-high"}`
