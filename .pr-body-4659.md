fix(seat): honour spawn-bench in comeback-release so a 402 wall is not re-probed

Closes #4659

## Why

`seat_usable` already holds a seat while `.spawn-bench.json` `usable_at` is in the future. Comeback-release only read the ledger's `bench_until // usable_at`. A PQE repair that wrote spawn-bench (7d hold on straitly/deepseek-v4-pro, usable_at 2026-09-12T18:47:00Z) therefore stopped routing but not re-probes. Lived 2026-09-09 05:31Z: the ledger's 1h PQE hold aged out, comeback-release probed, and a 402 from that probe (or from #3993 re-audition of retired Sol / unapproved qwen) re-anchors `observed_at` and retriggers FleetProviderQuotaExhausted.

Orchestrator decision: comeback-release must honour spawn-bench the same way `seat_usable` does. Do not re-probe a 402 wall. Do not top up straitly.

## Scope

- `bin/fleet-seat-comeback-release`: skip probe while spawn-bench `usable_at` is in the future; skip #3993 re-audition of an unlisted slug when another seat of the same provider already carries `source=money_boundary` / `quota_exhausted` / `quota_bench`; exclude those holds from the stalled and interval-breach loud checks.
- `tests/fleet-seat-comeback-release.test.sh` section 22: the lived fixture (listed 402 + future spawn-bench + unlisted sibling) must not probe and must not stall; the no-marker control still probes after the PQE window ages out.

No seat-caps change. No money-boundary raise. No straitly top-up.

## Blast Radius

The live 15-min comeback-release timer. A spawn-bench-held 402 is no longer probed or counted as stalled. An expired 402 with no spawn-bench still probes (22c). A lone unlisted slug with no sibling wall still re-auditions (#3993 test 21).
