## feat(precedence-band): heartbeat canary + ratchet for the rent-paying band (fleet-ops#1223)

Ledger 2026-08-27 | Precedence band + overnight machinery surge (Nish):

  - **Until cutoff_utc** (`2026-08-28T02:30:00Z`): surge — fleet-ops intake claims only `surge_leverage_issues`. Low-leverage machinery does NOT ride the surge.
  - **From cutoff_utc**: blanket fleet-precedence ends. Machinery is capped at `machinery_max_pct` (<=30) of live `pi-issue@` units; the product gets the rest, with `product_front` claimed first.
  - **Multiplier escape**: a machinery issue jumps the band only by a `band-multiplier: N` line on its body (sourced via `lib/precedence-band.sh` `precedence_band_has_multiplier`).
  - **Dial owner**: Weekly Fleet Review. The dial tightens only — a loosening of `machinery_max_pct` / `product_min_pct` vs the prior committed value requires a strictly-later `wfr_waiver_on` in the live config. A re-stamped waiver is rejected (fresh WFR required).
